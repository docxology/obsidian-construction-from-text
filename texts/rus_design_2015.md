### **2.2 Energy Transduction**

*   **Vector ID:** M2.2
*   **Vector Type:** Transduction
    *   Content: For pneumatic/hydraulic systems, electrical energy (compressor/pump) or chemical energy (pneumatic battery) is transduced into mechanical energy in the form of fluid pressure. This pressure is then applied to Fluidic Elastomer Actuators (FEAs) or Pneumatic Artificial Muscles (PAMs), causing deformation (bending, extension, contraction) of the soft robot body. For tendon-based systems, electrical energy powers motors that pull tendons, leading to mechanical deformation. EAPs directly transduce electrical energy into mechanical strain. Phase change materials actuated by thermal energy from embedded heaters convert thermal energy to mechanical stiffness changes.
    *   CT-GIN Mapping: `EnergyTransductionEdge`: attributes - `mechanism: Pneumatic/Hydraulic Expansion, Muscle-like Contraction, Tendon Tension, Electroactive Polymer Strain, Phase Change`, `from_node: Electrical Energy, Chemical Energy`, `to_node: Mechanical Deformation`

        *  Justification: The paper explicitly describes the different actuation methods and the energy transformations involved in each, including pneumatic, hydraulic, tendon and EAP based systems.
