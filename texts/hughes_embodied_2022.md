## M16: CT-GIN Template Self-Improvement Insights

*   **Vector ID:** M16
*   **Vector Type:** Feedback

### **Template Feedback:**

*    **Vector ID:** M16.1
*   **Vector Type:** Text
Provide specific, actionable feedback on the *CT-GIN template itself*, based on this analysis:
    *   **Missing Probes:** For review papers, a probe to assess the paper's *methodology for review* (systematic, meta-analysis, etc.) might be useful. Also, for review papers particularly, probes on the paper's *critical stance* towards the field, and identification of biases or perspectives embedded within the review itself could be added.
    *   **Unclear Definitions:** The definition of "Implementation Clarity" may be slightly ambiguous for review papers, as 'implementation' isn't directly applicable. Rephrasing to 'Conceptual Clarity' for review papers might be better.
    *   **Unclear Node/Edge Representations:** For review papers, the CT-GIN mapping guidance needs to be adapted to conceptual relationships rather than direct material system components. Examples for mapping abstract concepts and research areas would improve clarity for review paper analysis.
    *   **Scoring Difficulties:** Scoring "Cognitive Proximity" in a review, where no specific system is demonstrated, is challenging. The scale needs clearer anchors for review papers that are evaluating field progress rather than individual systems. Maybe levels could be adapted to "Level of Cognitive Ambition of the Field as Represented in the Review".
    *   **Data Extraction/Output Mapping:** Extracting "data" from a review is inherently different. The template could benefit from specific guidance on extracting *qualitative findings* and *conceptual relationships* from review paper text and mapping these into CT-GIN elements.
    *   **Overall Usability:** The template is quite detailed, which is good for thorough analysis, but can be time-consuming for review papers that are conceptually oriented. Perhaps a slightly simplified version or a 'review paper pathway' within the template could improve usability for this paper type.
    * **Specific Suggestions:**
        * Add "Review Methodology" section to Module M11.
        * Refine "Implementation Clarity" to "Conceptual Clarity" for review papers.
        * Provide specific examples for CT-GIN mapping for conceptual elements in review papers (e.g., research directions, disciplines).
        * Adjust "Cognitive Proximity Scale" with review paper specific anchors, focusing on field's cognitive ambitions.
        * Add guidance on extracting qualitative findings and conceptual relationships from review papers for CT-GIN mapping.
        * Consider a simplified template pathway for review paper analysis to enhance usability.
        * Add probes for 'Critical Stance' and 'Perspective Bias' to Module M11.
