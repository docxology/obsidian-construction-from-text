## M1: System Overview & Implementation
*   **Vector ID:** M1

### **1.1 System Description**

*   **Vector ID:** M1.1
*   **Vector Type:** Description
    *   Content: The system is a voxel-based virtual soft robot simulated in 2D using the EvoGym simulator. The robot is composed of voxels with different material properties: four types of sensory voxels responding to two binary stimuli (expanding or contracting in presence or absence of each stimulus), two active voxels providing locomotion through sinusoidal expansion and contraction, and two passive voxels (soft and rigid). The robot's morphology is generated by a Compositional Pattern Producing Network (CPPN) and evolved using a Map-Elites algorithm to perform adaptive locomotion. The objective is to demonstrate adaptive behavior (changing direction) in response to environmental stimuli without a neural network controller, relying solely on morphological computation.
    *   CT-GIN Mapping: `SystemNode` attributes: `systemType: virtual soft robot`, `domain: robotics, morphological computation`, `mechanism: voxel-based simulation, evolutionary algorithm, reactive materials`, `components: sensory voxels, active voxels, passive voxels, CPPN, Map-Elites algorithm, EvoGym simulator`, `purpose: demonstrate adaptive locomotion via morphological computation`.

        *  Justification: The system components, simulation environment, and purpose are explicitly described in Sections 1 and 2. The concept of "morphological computation" as the driving mechanism is explicitly stated in the Abstract and Introduction, while the specific details of the voxel types and their reactive behaviors are also explicitly outlined in Section 2.
